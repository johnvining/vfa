# Vining Family Association — Bibliography & Materials

A working list of books, printed resources, and physical artifacts relevant to Vining family genealogy. Tracks ownership and location where known so we know who to ask for a copy or scan. Add new entries as they come up.

## Books

### The Vining Family
- **Author**: Dorothea (Vining) Barnes

### Life Goes On
- **Author**: Elberta Dale Vining (née Dale)
- **Type**: Personal memoir
- **Genealogical use**: Source for the marriage date of Virgil Vernon Vining and Elberta Dale (10 March 1946); likely contains other firsthand details about the Virgil Vernon Vining family.
- **Known copies**:
  - John Vining (current website maintainer; Virgil and Elberta's grandson).

### Not Without Them: A Family History of the Carter, Terry, Vining, and Williams Families
- **Author**: Joan Terry Williams Lafleur

## Physical Materials

### Abington Vining Family Bible
- **Type**: Family Bible
- **Original bookseller's description**:
  > This appears very similar to the late 18th-century Isaac Collins printings, from Trenton, New Jersey, however it is missing its title pages, which may have been used as pastedowns in an unprofessional repair. This family bible belonged to the Vining and Hunt families, including Asa and Sarah (Hunt) Vining, and their children Martin, David, and Sally Vining of Hawley, Massachusetts. Sarah Vining's parents are mentioned as well, Experience and Thomas Hunt of Abington, Massachusetts. Original leather binding re-covered with leather, and unprofessionally stitched. Some pages appear to have been glued underneath the repair stitching. Leather is damaged and brittle. Unpaginated pages, old and new testaments with publisher's margin notes and index. Pages are brittle, foxed, and spotted, and corners are greatly rubbed. Some pages may be loose. Contains dirt, dust, and a few pressed flowers. If this bible is from Isaac Collins, this publisher was the first to publish a bible in New Jersey, which became the second quarto King James Bible printed in America. It was considered one of the most typographically accurate Bibles of its day. Isaac Collins, a Quaker printer and bookseller, commissioned a series of committees led by John Witherspoon to correct his proof sheets in order to ensure the text was error free. It is unclear if this copy ever included Witherspoon's address to the reader; if so, it is missing. It does include a dedication page to King James. Hardcover, acceptable condition. Unpaginated, quarto. Seller Inventory # 1716253

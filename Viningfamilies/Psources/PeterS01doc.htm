Notes and Documentation for Peter Smith Vining - Sarah Maria Hicks


Notes

A Carolyn Vining was reported to be a member of this household by Dorothea Vining Barnes in her 1980 book The Vining Family. Based on that book, she is included here but her presence in the census record has not been found yet.

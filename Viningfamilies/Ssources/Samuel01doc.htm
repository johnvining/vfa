Documentation for Samuel Vining - Sarah Devril


Research Opportunities

1. Samuel Vining, son of immigrant ancester John, and wife Sarah Devril appear to be the first Vinings to move away from Weymouth, Norfolk County, Massachusetts. Samuel and Sarah’s first three children were born in Weymouth, but their fourth, Colaty, was born in Braintree, Norfolk County, Massachusetts.To be determined is when they (including the first three children?) moved to Braintree.

2. Samuel and Sarah died in Connecticut. Oldest son Samuel Jr. likely died in Connecticut, and daughter Colaty married in Connecticut. To be determined is if Samuel, Sarah, and their four children all moved to Connecticut at the same time and, if so, where.

Note

The “Gorham P. Vining House” (at 1590 Oakridge Road in Neenah, Winnebago County, Wisconsin) is listed on both the National Register of Historic Places and the (Wisconsin) State Register.



It was built in 1848 in Greek Revival style and was added to in 1857.

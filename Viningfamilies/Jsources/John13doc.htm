Research Opportunity

John's son Thomas was not mentioned in his father's will, but Zachariah, Thomas's son, was mentioned. Also, Jesse was named as John's “youngest son”. This suggests that John's son Thomas predeceased his father's making of his will, which was 26 April 1801. Research is needed regarding this possibility.

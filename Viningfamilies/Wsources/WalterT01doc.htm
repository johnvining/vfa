Note:

From a letter written by Bertha A. Vining on 17 October 1966:
   “Taught school in 1st and 4th grades of Kankakee Illinois schools + took nurse's training in St. Luke's (Episcopal) Hospital 14th & Michigan Ave., Chicago, — 1908 — graduated 1911. Practiced Registered Nursing as an Industrial Nurse before retiring from Illinois Bell Telephone Company's medical department. Am a veteran of U.S.A.N.C. in World War I. A member of the American Legion Post #270 of Chicago, Ill. — charter member — I served in France.”

The Vining Family Association received a large collection of studio and family photographs of the Vining/Hargraves families of Coffee County, Georgia, c. 1890-1920, from Dr. Antoinette Graham, a retiring antiques dealer of Florida. There are also materials related to Pinellas County, Florida.

One photograph has been identified: <a href="Viningfamilies/Jfamilies.htm#James04">James Vining</a> and his wife, Sarah Brooker Vining, of Coffee County, Georgia, c. 1890.

The majority of the photographs are unidentified or bear only a first name. Three of the larger studio photographs have been posted on the <a href="Viningresearchproblemshome.htm">Research Problems</a> page. If you are familiar with this family and would be interested in helping us identify these photographs, please contact <a href="mailto:genealogy@vining-family.org">genealogy@vining-family.org</a>.

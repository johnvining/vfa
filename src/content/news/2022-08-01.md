<b>Vining Essay Contest</b> — The Vining Family Association announces an essay contest. The contest is open to anyone who is interested in writing a 1,500 maximum word essay on a Vining. The essay may be about a Vining of the writer&#8217;s choice and may be expository, narrative or descriptive in nature. The winning essayist will receive an Ancestry DNA Kit and have their essay published in the Vining Family Association&#8217;s Newsletter.<br>
Entries may be emailed to Judi A. Vining, <a href="mailto:judi.vining@aol.com">judi.vining@aol.com</a>, or sent by traditional mail to Judi A. Vining, P.O. Box 505, Yardley, PA 19067.
